On the design of these tools

A note on each tool in the collection — what traditional teaching method it replaces or improves on, and the cognitive mechanism behind why it works. In all cases I have strived for iPad compatibility first, as my students work on these devices, most especially pushing for handwriting compatibility as research has shown this to be more effective for learning than typing.
