The wave shows the direction the light oscillates in, not its true size: real light waves are far too small to see. Positive angles are clockwise as the observer sees them, looking through the eyepiece towards the lamp. The (+) and (−) enantiomers use an example specific rotation of ±60°; the sucrose value is the measured one.
