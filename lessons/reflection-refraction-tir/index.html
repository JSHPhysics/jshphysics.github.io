Reflection, refraction and total internal reflection

A ray diagram is not a picture of a light beam. It is a record of what a ray did when it met a surface, with every angle measured from the normal. Here a ray box turns slowly while a pen records the angle out against the angle in — so the law of reflection, refraction and the critical angle each show up as a shape on the graph.
